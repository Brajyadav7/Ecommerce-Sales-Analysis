# 📊 E-Commerce Data Analytics Project

This project analyzes an E-commerce dataset to extract business insights using **Python, SQL, and Data Visualization**.  
The goal is to understand customer behavior, sales performance, and revenue trends to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🚀 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales and revenue trends
- Identify top-performing products
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Build visual dashboards for business insights
- Apply advanced analytics like **Customer Segmentation and RFM Analysis**

## ⭐ Advanced Analytics

### Customer Segmentation

Customers can be segmented into groups based on purchasing behavior:

- High Value Customers
- Regular Customers
- Occasional Buyers
- Churn Risk Customers

This helps businesses improve **targeted marketing strategies**.

---

### RFM Analysis

RFM stands for:

- **Recency** → How recently a customer purchased
- **Frequency** → How often they purchase
- **Monetary** → How much money they spend

Using RFM scoring, customers are classified into segments such as:

- Champions
- Loyal Customers
- Potential Loyalists
- At Risk
- Lost Customers
